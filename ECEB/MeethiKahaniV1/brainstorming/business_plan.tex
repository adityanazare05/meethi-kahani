\section{Executive Summary \& Brand Strategy}
\subsection{Business Concept}
\textbf{Meethi Kahani} (Mithi Kahani) is a premium Direct-To-Consumer (D2C) artisanal cookie bakery brand founded in Mumbai. The company specializes in 100\% Pure Eggless, handcrafted cookies baked fresh daily using traditional recipes, high-quality butter, desi ghee, and premium ingredients.

\subsection{Core Brand Vision \& Positioning}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{100\% Eggless Guarantee:} Crucial brand USP catering to the massive Indian vegetarian market.
    \item \textbf{2-Year Single Product Focus:} 100\% focus on Cookies for Years 1--2 (Fudges and Bakery items reserved for Year 2+ expansion).
    \item \textbf{Visual Identity:} Deep Crimson Red (\#9A2222) and Vanilla Linen Cream (\#FAF5EE) sampled directly from the official logo.
    \item \textbf{Brand Motto:} Strict NO-POPUP Policy for an ultra-clean, serene, luxury e-commerce experience.
\end{itemize}

\section{Market Segmentation \& Purchasing Tracks}
\subsection{Customer Segments}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Personal Track (\texttt{/}):} Everyday single consumers ordering fresh cookie boxes for personal cravings.
    \item \textbf{Gifting Track (\texttt{/gifting}):} Curated gift tins and Build-Your-Own gift boxes featuring custom wax-sealed greeting cards.
    \item \textbf{Corporate Track (\texttt{/corporate}):} B2B sample requests and bulk quote inquiry forms.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Pricing \& Discount Architecture}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Order types (Personal, Gifting, Corporate) are distinct purchase categories, NOT discount tags.
    \item Discounts are strictly promo codes created by Admin in the Offers Manager (e.g. \texttt{MEETHI20}).
\end{itemize}

\section{Core System Architecture \& Data Logic}
\subsection{Single Master Stock Architecture}
All customer orders (Personal, Gifting, Corporate) decrement stock from a single master database table (\texttt{Cookie\_Stock}). This eliminates duplicate inventory entries and stock sync errors.

\subsection{Frontend Tech Stack}
React 18 + Vite + Pure Vanilla CSS3 + React Router DOM + Lucide React Icons. Hosted as a lightweight Single-Page Application (SPA) on Vercel/Netlify.

\section{Website Structure \& UI/UX Blueprint}
\subsection{Centered Logo Header Layout}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Left:} Hamburger Menu Icon (\texttt{🍔}).
    \item \textbf{Center:} Meethi Kahani Logo.
    \item \textbf{Right:} Account Icon (\texttt{👤}) \& Cart Icon (\texttt{🛒}) with live item counter badge.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Key Page Components}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Freshness Ticker:} \textit{"🔥 [X] Boxes left -- Freshly baked in the last 3 hours"}.
    \item \textbf{Dedicated Product Details Page (\texttt{/product/:slug}):} Left side 3-4 photo gallery; Right side Title, Price, Add to Cart, Pincode Checker, and \textit{"The Kahani Behind This Cookie"} at the very bottom.
    \item \textbf{Slide-Over Cart Drawer:} Cookie Crumb Progress Bar for free shipping.
    \item \textbf{Customer Review Carousel:} Sliding card carousel right above the Footer.
    \item \textbf{Pixel-Perfect Dummy Razorpay Gateway:} Simulated UPI QR scan and Card payments.
\end{itemize}

\section{Secret Admin \& Seller Portals}
\subsection{Super Admin Portal (\texttt{/admin})}
Protected by UserID and Password. Includes Hero Banner Carousel Manager, Offers/Coupons Manager, 2-Level Product Catalog, Campaign Creator with \texttt{\{Customer\_Name\}} dynamic parsing, Style B GST Tax Invoice Generator, Store Open/Pause Master Switch, and Low Stock Alerts.

\subsection{Seller / Bakery Kitchen Portal (\texttt{/seller})}
Protected by UserID and Password. Includes Live Kitchen Order Queue (\texttt{Received} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{Baking} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{Shipped} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{Delivered}) and Packaging Material Inventory Manager.

\subsection{Dedicated Heavy Analytics Route (\texttt{/admin/analysis})}
Isolated on \texttt{/admin/analysis} to keep main site fast. Includes the 9 Master Analytics Modules:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{\#1 Stock \& Baking Behavior Analytics} (Week-to-Week \& Month-to-Month)
    \item \textbf{Customer Demographics Analytics} (Mumbai MMR Sub-Zones: South Mumbai, Western Suburbs, Eastern Suburbs, Navi Mumbai, Thane, Kalyan-Dombivli)
    \item Financial \& Revenue Analytics
    \item Product Flavor Velocity Analytics
    \item Promo \& Campaign ROI Analytics
    \item Customer Retention Analytics (\texttt{Kisse} Coins activity)
    \item Delivery \& Logistics Analytics
    \item Cart \& Checkout Funnel Analytics
    \item Customer Review Rating Analytics
\end{enumerate}

\section{Loyalty Program: Kahani Coins (Kisse)}
Customers earn 1 \textbf{"Kissa"} coin for every \rupee 10 spent. Accumulated \textit{Kisse} can be redeemed for discounts or free cookie boxes on future orders.

\section{Database Schema Specifications}
\subsection{Master Database Tables}
The platform utilizes 11 core relational tables: \texttt{Customers}, \texttt{Admin\_Sellers}, \texttt{Categories}, \texttt{Cookie\_Stock}, \texttt{Packaging\_Stock}, \texttt{Hero\_Banners}, \texttt{Coupons}, \texttt{Orders}, \texttt{Order\_Items}, \texttt{Serviceable\_Pincodes}, and \texttt{Customer\_Reviews}.

\subsection{Standardized Address Schema}
Addresses utilize 6 distinct fields (\texttt{address\_line\_1}, \texttt{address\_line\_2}, \texttt{town}, \texttt{city}, \texttt{pincode}, \texttt{state}) for fast SQL \texttt{GROUP BY town} regional analytics.
