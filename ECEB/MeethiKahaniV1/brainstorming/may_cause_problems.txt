[DECISION UPDATE: Corporate B2B customer track removed from scope. 
This simplifies the platform strictly to B2C Personal & Gifting tracks and eliminates all Corporate B2B risks (Multi-address shipping, 30-day PO credit cycles, GSTIN B2B invoices, logo lead times, & corporate stock wipeout).]

1. SINGLE STOCK POOL INVENTORY RISKS
-----------------------------------
• Risk 1: Gifting Surge Draining Personal Stock
  - Problem: A sudden surge in B2C Gifting Box orders could drain cookie stock available for single B2C buyers.
  - Solution Needed: Low-stock threshold alerts in Seller Portal (`/seller`) & real-time inventory count checks before checkout.

2. NO-REFUND POLICY & FOOD SAFETY FRICTION
------------------------------------------
• Risk 2: Courier Transit Damage Complaints
  - Problem: Food safety regulations mandate a strict NO-RETURNS / NO-REFUNDS policy, but cookies damaged in courier transit will cause bad customer reviews.
  - Solution Needed: "Damage Replacement Policy" (Send fresh replacement box upon photo proof within 24 hours, instead of cash refund).
